Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to PiNexusCommerce Cloud. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our AI-powered global commerce discovery and research application.

1. Purpose of the Application

PiNexusCommerce Cloud is designed to help buyers, sellers, suppliers, manufacturers, resellers and traders discover and research publicly available commerce information.

The application may provide links to original third-party websites. Transactions, purchases and other activities completed on third-party websites are governed by those websites' respective terms and privacy policies.

2. Information You Provide

When using the application, you may voluntarily provide information such as search queries, product requirements, quantities, destination information, budgets and other commerce-related requirements.

Please do not submit passwords, private keys, wallet seed phrases, passphrases or other confidential security credentials.

3. Public Web Information

The application may use publicly available information from external websites and search services to help answer commerce research requests.

Information obtained from external websites remains subject to the policies and terms of those respective websites.

4. AI-Assisted Analysis

AI may be used to understand search requests, organize publicly available information and provide research or analysis.

AI-generated information may contain errors or estimates. Information should be independently verified before making commercial, financial or purchasing decisions.

5. Third-Party Websites

PiNexusCommerce Cloud may provide links to original websites, marketplaces, suppliers or other external services.

We do not control the privacy practices, content, security or policies of third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of those websites before using them.

6. Pi Network

Where Pi Network functionality is officially integrated, Pi-related interactions will be handled through the official Pi Network mechanisms supported by the application.

PiNexusCommerce Cloud will not request or store a user's wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children who are unable to independently use online commerce and research services.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, technology or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning PiNexusCommerce Cloud, users should use the contact method provided by the application or its official project channels.

11. User Responsibility

PiNexusCommerce Cloud provides research and information tools. Users remain responsible for verifying information and making their own final commercial decisions.

By using PiNexusCommerce Cloud, you acknowledge that information from public sources and AI-assisted analysis may change and should be independently verified.
